Dreams Beyond Reality — a deterministic TypeScript program that paints a giant whale swimming among the clouds above a small glowing village, and writes it as a 1024×1024 PNG.

## Scene

A colossal whale drifts diagonally through a deep indigo sky, lit by a warm horizon and a pale moon, its body traced with faint bioluminescent spots. Far behind it, a second smaller whale fades into the mist. Below, tucked into the clouds, a tiny village glows — dark rooftops, warm windows, and a lane of lanterns.

## Run it

```sh
npx tsx main.ts
```
